# Account and Data Deletion

**Last Updated:** 01.09.2026

At **Wroclaw Dwarf Hunt**, we take your privacy seriously. To protect your identity, we use anonymous authentication via Apple and Google. **We do not collect, know, or store your email address.**

Because we do not have your email, we cannot process deletion requests via standard email verification (as we have no way to prove which account belongs to you).

To delete your account and all associated data, please choose one of the following methods:

### Method 1: The Easiest Way (In-App)
If you still have the app installed, you can delete your data instantly:
1. Open the Wroclaw Dwarf Hunt app.
2. Go to **Settings** -> **Account** -> **Advanced**.
3. Tap **Delete Account** and confirm.
*This will immediately and permanently erase your profile, progress, and unlink your authentication token from our servers.*

### Method 2: Manual Request via Support ID
If you have uninstalled the app but previously saved your `Support ID` (can be found in the App Settings: **Settings** -> **Account** -> **Advanced**), you can request manual deletion:
* Send an email to **dwarfshunt@gmail.com** with the subject **"Account Deletion Request"**.
* Include your exact `Support ID` in the body of the email.
* We will delete your data within 7 days.

### Method 3: If you uninstalled the app and lost your Support ID
Because your account is completely anonymous and tied only to a secure token generated by your device, we cannot identify your account without the Support ID.
To delete your data, you will need to:
1. Reinstall the app.
2. Sign in with the exact same Google or Apple account you used previously.
3. Follow the steps in **Method 1** to delete your account from within the settings.

---
**What happens when you delete your account?**
Your display name, leaderboard points, and the link to your Apple/Google token will be permanently destroyed. Any photos of dwarfs you submitted may be retained completely anonymously to improve our image recognition system, but they will no longer be linked to you in any way.
